Student Activities Management System (SAMS)

Main Data Flows

Student → SAMS
- Browse Activities
- Register for Activities
- Cancel Registration

SAMS → Student
- Notifications
- Activity History
- Activity Transcript

Activity Organizer → SAMS
- Create Activity
- Update Activity
- Record Attendance

SAMS → Activity Organizer
- Participant List
- Activity Reports

Student Affairs Department → SAMS
- Approve Activities
- Manage Activity Categories

SAMS → Student Affairs Department
- Statistics
- Administrative Reports

University Academic System → SAMS
- Student Information
- Graduation Status

SAMS → University Academic System
- Activity Transcript
