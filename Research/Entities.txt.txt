Student Activities Management System (SAMS)

External Entities

1. Student
- Browse activities
- Register
- Cancel registration
- View activity history
- Receive notifications

2. Activity Organizer
- Create activity
- Update activity
- Record attendance
- View participant list

3. Student Affairs Department
- Approve activities
- Manage categories
- Generate reports

4. University Academic System
- Provide student information
- Receive activity transcript
